Being heard

Your voice carries ~50 m. Further off, only the drummer can beat your order down the line, two seconds late. Lose him and you must walk to your men.

Firing

B Fire by rank — the front rank fires, then the next, so some muskets are always loaded.

5 Make ready — the front two ranks present and hold. F / Space FIRE — everyone presented lets go at once. A volley close in breaks companies; at 150 m it wastes powder. Reloading takes about a minute. 6 lets each man fire when he has a mark. Men only fire when halted.

Moving

1 Follow me — a column that walks in your footsteps. 2 Form line. 3 Advance (wheels to face your mouse). 4 Halt. 9 Fall back, facing the enemy. 8 Skirmish order — loose, hard to hit, easy to ride over. 0 Column of attack — narrow and deep: it barely fires and suffers more, but its charge hits far harder. Skirmish, column of attack and fire by rank are learned at camp.

Nerve

Men fall back when shot, faster when it comes all at once or from the flank. The colours, the music and you nearby steady them. R Rally — in person — can turn a rout.

The bayonet

C Charge. Wait until they waver. Most charges are won before they arrive.
